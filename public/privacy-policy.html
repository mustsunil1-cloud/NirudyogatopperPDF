Privacy Policy

Nirudyoaga Topper PDF · Last updated September 2026

This app does not require an account. Your selected PDF stays on your device unless you choose Quiz or Explain. When you do, the PDF is sent over the internet to the app's analysis service and forwarded to Google Gemini (and optionally Groq) to produce the result.

The analysis service processes the PDF to respond to your request and does not save uploaded PDFs as files. Google and Groq may process content under their own privacy terms. Do not submit sensitive or confidential documents unless you are authorized and comfortable with this processing.

The app saves your language, theme, and AI provider preferences on your device. It does not collect your name, email address, or location. Network providers and service providers may process technical data needed to deliver the analysis.

For questions about this policy, contact the app developer (Sunilkumar Kotrangi). This policy may be updated as the app changes.
